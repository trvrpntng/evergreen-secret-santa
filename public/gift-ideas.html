Christmas
gift ideas.

Think about what your match enjoys, then pick something that fits your group’s gift budget.

We may earn a commission if you buy through one of the shopping links below.

  • A cosy night in

    Pick thick socks, a ceramic mug or a hot chocolate kit. Add a handwritten recipe for your favourite film-night snack.

  • Something delicious

    Try a local jam, a spice blend or a coffee sampler. Check dietary needs before choosing a treat.

  • A game to share

    A compact card game or a quiz deck is easy to bring to a gathering. A pocket puzzle is a good pick for someone who likes a solo challenge.

  • A good read

    Look for a short story collection, an illustrated guide to their hobby or a paperback from a genre they enjoy.

  • A brighter desk

    A pocket notebook, a few good pens or a tidy cable organiser can make the working day a little nicer.

  • A day to look forward to

    Try an independent cinema voucher, a pottery workshop or coffee for two. Look for a gift they can use on a date that suits them.
